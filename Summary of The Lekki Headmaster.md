Summary of The Lekki Headmaster
The story revolves around Bepo, a committed teacher at Stardom Schools, as he navigates numerous challenges to improve students’ learning conditions.

The school’s Managing Director (MD) becomes suspicious of staff members living beyond their means, such as purchasing expensive cars, which raises concerns about potential financial mismanagement.
Tensions escalate when the Stardom Cooperative Society reports significant financial reserves, leading the MD to limit staff loans.
A critical incident occurs when a parent spots a grammatical error in a note written by Fafore, the school’s English teacher. This sparks outrage, questioning the school’s academic standards.
Bepo finds himself in a moral dilemma when he discovers unethical practices by the school director that could negatively impact the students. He must decide whether to stay loyal or expose these actions, knowing the risks involved.

Themes Explored
Ethics and Integrity: The novel explores the challenges of upholding ethical standards in a flawed system.
Authority and Accountability: It examines the responsibilities of leaders in ensuring the success of educational institutions.
Challenges in Education: The story highlights the struggles of maintaining quality education amidst societal and financial pressures.
