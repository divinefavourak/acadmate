## Instructions for the LLM (read before writing)

You are writing a post for **Acadmate**, a Nigerian student platform. Audience: secondary
school leavers and undergraduates preparing for exams, hunting scholarships, and planning
careers. Write in clear, warm, direct English. Short paragraphs. Talk to the reader as "you."
No fluff, no filler, no "in today's fast-paced world" openers. Be specific and practical.

Acadmate is NOT only about JAMB/UTME. It also covers school news, scholarships, study tips,
and careers — keep that breadth in mind so the blog feels like a full student companion.

Fill every field below. Do not change the field names. Do not add fields.

### How to publish after pasting
1. Go to `/admin/blog` and click **New post**.
2. Paste Title, Excerpt, Body, choose the Category, set the URL slug (or leave blank).
3. Upload the cover image you sourced from the description above.
4. Click **Save draft**, switch to the **Preview** tab to check formatting.
5. Click **Publish** when ready. (Heads-up: publishing emails Premium users once — it won't
   resend if you re-publish later.)
